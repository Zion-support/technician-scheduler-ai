Technician Scheduling Preparation

Prepare a clear visit brief for a human coordinator. This is a planning resource, not an AI scheduling service.

Prepare the evidence

  1. Record the site, ticket, scope, requested window and named client contact.
  2. Review skills, tools, access restrictions, travel and written availability.
  3. Confirm complete written costs and applicable terms with an accountable owner.
  4. Obtain client confirmation, applicable purchase order and separate written Zion authorization before reservation, travel or dispatch.
  5. Record a review date and stop if scope, access, costs or authority remain unclear.
